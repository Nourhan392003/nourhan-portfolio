Profile image — replace with your real photo
============================================

The hero currently uses `profile.svg`, an abstract placeholder that keeps the
frame's 4:5 vertical composition so the layout and entrance animation can be
reviewed.

To use your real photo:

1. Drop your image in this folder named `profile.jpg` (or update the `src`
   in index.html to your filename, e.g. `images/nourhan.jpg`).
2. Recommended source: a portrait photo, roughly 4:5 ratio (e.g. 800x1000),
   face in the upper half / center. The frame uses `object-fit: cover` with
   `object-position: center top`, so the face stays visible and is never
   cropped away or distorted.
3. Keep the filename without spaces. A JPEG/WebP at ~1200px wide is plenty —
   the hero shows it at ~420px.
